Shaheedi Bunga

The place of the martyrs.

Amritsar

Around this sarovar stood more than seventy bungas.

Raised by the misls and the jathas who kept watch over the Darbar Sahib. Each one was a residence — and each one was a school.

What was taught

Santhiya. Shastar vidya. Itihaas. Kirtan.

A bunga was where a Sikh came to learn to read Gurbani correctly, to hold a shastar, to keep the rehat, and to sing the raags as they were given.

What happened to them

Almost all of them were pulled down.

Cleared through the last century as the parikrama was widened. Of more than seventy, a handful of towers still stand at the sarovar’s edge.

The one we are named for

Shaheedan da Bunga.

The bunga of the shaheedi jatha — the Singhs who gave their heads for the Panth, and taught in between. The building is gone. The teaching was never the building.

Students teaching worldwide

So we raise it again, on whatever ground we are given.

A gurdwara hall in Leicester on a Tuesday night. A vidyala in Panjab. And students who learned here, now teaching where they live. A bunga is not a place. It is what happens inside it.

03 — What is taught

ਖੜਾ ਹੋਣਾStance

Feet, hips, shoulders. Before the bow is even lifted, the body is squared to the target and the weight is settled. Most of a bad shot happens here.

ਸਾਹBreath

The draw happens on the inhale, the loose on the held breath. Archery is a breathing discipline that happens to involve a bow.

ਧਿਆਨFocus

One ring. Not the target — one ring, and then one point inside it. The eye goes where the arrow will.

ਛੋੜਨਾRelease

The loose is not a decision, it is a letting go. Force it and the arrow knows. This is the part that takes years.

ਮੁਹਾਰਤMastery

Dead centre, repeatedly, without thinking about any of the above. Nobody arrives; you only get closer.

04 — When

Sundays, 2.30–4pm — archery and shastar vidya at Guru Tegh Bahadur Gurdwara, 106 East Park Road, Leicester LE5 4QB. Air rifling runs Saturday afternoons at the same hall.

Bows, arrows and safety equipment are provided. Ages 14 and over on Sundays; younger archers shoot at camps and the Sikhi Camp tag-archery sessions.

Half past two at East Park Road. You do not need a bow, and you do not need to have held one.

Kirtan Vidyala

A test of commitment.

Kirtan, learned on the instruments it was written for — dilruba, taus, rabab and saranda.

None of it comes quickly. It asks real commitment and determination. Gurmukhi is not needed to start, but it becomes necessary later, for shabads and Asa di Vaar.

Classes run nine times a week at Gipsy Lane — every weekday morning, two evenings, and both days of the weekend — and every one of them is open to any age. You do not join a term or wait for one to start; you come to the next class. There is no fee and no audition.

There are instruments to learn on, so you do not need your own to begin. You can get one later, once you know you will keep going.
