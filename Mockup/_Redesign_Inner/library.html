Library

Made here.

The Resources page holds the granths and pothis the vidyala reads from. The Library holds what the vidhiarti, our students, have made from them: notes, charts, translations and study aids, written here and left for whoever comes next.

The shelf is empty for now while we collect the first pieces. If you have made something that belongs on it, please tell us.

Watch and listen

Much of the katha, talks and kirtan at Shaheedi Bunga is recorded, and you can watch it for free on our YouTube channel, @shaheedibunga.

The larivaar katha of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji goes up almost every day. There are more than 280 episodes so far, taken in order of the angs, and the katha has now passed Ang 570.

Alongside it are 77 Gurmat vichar talks, 52 recordings of raag kirtan, and 32 videos for anyone learning kirtan, tabla or jori.
