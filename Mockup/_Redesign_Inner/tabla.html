Tabla & Jori

Learn to play Tabla and Jori, and to keep the Taal that Kirtan rests on.

ThekaTeentaal, 16 matraWhereLocally and onlineFeeNone

What is taught

Guru Nanak Dev Ji says: let understanding be the instrument, and love the pakhavaj. A pakhavaj is a drum, and the Taal is its heartbeat. In this class you learn the theka, the basic pattern of beats, that keeps Kirtan steady, and you learn to listen as closely as you play.

Beginners are welcome. You start with the bols and the theka, and work up to accompanying Kirtan in the Taals the Kirtan Vidyala sings in. Jori is taught alongside Tabla.

What we have run before

  • Santhiya and Kirtan classes at Gurdwara Sri Guru Dasmesh Sahib, Leicester LE4 6RB, with Tabla and Jori for beginners (as posted on our Instagram).
  • A weekly round-up of Gurbani, Kirtan, Santhiya, Gatka, Tabla, Jori and Itihaas classes at gurdwaras across Leicester.
  • The six-week Santhiya camp in summer 2025, where students learnt Kirtan and Tabla alongside Santhiya.
  • A five-day Sikhi Camp in October 2025, with Tabla and Jori classes beside Dilruba, vaja and Santhiya.
