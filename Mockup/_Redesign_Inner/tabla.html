What is taught

Beginners are welcome. The class starts from the bols and the theka, and works towards accompanying kirtan in the taals the Kirtan Vidyala sings in. Contact us to inquire more about the level groups and who teaches.

From the record

  • Mon to Fri at Gurdwara Sri Guru Dasmesh Sahib, Leicester LE4 6RB: santhiya and kirtan classes, plus tabla and jori for beginners (from the charity's Instagram).
  • A weekly round-up of Gurbani, Kirtan, Santhiya, Gatka, tabla, Jori and Itihaas classes across Leicester's gurdware.
