Through the yearThe year at Shaheedi Bunga

Most of the events in the archive below come round every year, at roughly these times.

February to May. Gurbani Santhiya Camps at Gurudwara Shri Guru Dashmesh Sahib in the half-term and Easter holidays.

March. The Patshahi Dasvi National Hola Mohalla UK, with Gatka and tag archery tournaments.

The school holidays. The Sikhi Camp for children aged 5 to 18. Contact us to ask about the next one.

August. The Gurbani Vidhya Camp, two weeks at Guru Tegh Bahadur Gurdwara reading one Granth through.

Late November or early December. The 25 Singh Lareevaar Sri Akhand Paath Samagam, over three days. It has been held every winter since 2021.
