Guru Amar Das Ji calls the mind a mirror, and says a Gurmukh looks into it. A photograph is a smaller mirror. It holds still, for a moment, the low tables and the faces of a Sangat, and lets you look for as long as you like.

Children appear in some of these photographs. If your child is in one and you would rather they were not, email us and we will take it down. You do not have to give a reason.

On InstagramEight years on Instagram

We have posted on Instagram since February 2018, and the account now holds more than 750 posts and reels of camps, Samagams, Gatka, Kirtan and the weekly classes. Each camp and Samagam is posted there first.

The larger Samagams are also on YouTube, streamed live from the arambh to the bhog.
