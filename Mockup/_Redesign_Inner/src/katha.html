What is taught

You learn to read a Shabad closely, work out what it is saying, and explain it to a Sangat plainly and correctly.

In 2019 students were learning Katha of Sri Gur Partap Suraj Granth with us, and students from the class gave Katha on stage.

Larivaar Katha of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji goes out on our YouTube channel almost every day. Each episode carries on from the Ang where the last one stopped. Episode 284, on 27 October 2026, reached Ang 571.

The channel also has Katha of other Granths, including Adhyatam Parkash, added in September 2026.
