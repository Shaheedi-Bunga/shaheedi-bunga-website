Your first week

How it starts.

There is no form and no waiting list. This is the whole of it.

੧

Send one message.

Tell us which class, or that you do not know yet. Someone who teaches it will reply with this week's times.

੨

Turn up.

Come a few minutes early, cover your head, and sit with the sangat. Instruments are lent to anyone without their own, and granths are shared.

੩

Learn from the first day.

You are taught at your own pace, by volunteers who learnt the same way. Nobody is paid, and nothing is charged. Then you keep coming.

Not sure where to start?

Ask us. Someone will tell you where to turn up.

Santhiya is where everything else at the vidyala starts. Whichever class you choose, it begins with one message.
