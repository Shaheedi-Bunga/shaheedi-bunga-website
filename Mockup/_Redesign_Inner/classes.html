Gurmat Vidyala · Classes

ਗੁਰਮਤਿ ਵਿਦਿਆਲਾ

Come and learn with us.

Gurmat classes at gurdwaras in Leicester. Every one is free, and every teacher is a volunteer.

VidyaleSixWhereTwo gurdwarasFeeNone

How it works

Almost every class is open to all ages. There is no registration and no audition, and you take part from your first session.

The vidyale

The six classes.

Each class has its own page. Message us on WhatsApp for this week's times.

01

Kirtan Vidyalaਕੀਰਤਨ ਵਿਦਿਆਲਾ

Learn kirtan on the instruments it was written for: dilruba, taus, rabab and saranda.Nine classes a week at Gurudwara Shri Guru Dashmesh Sahib, Gipsy Lane. Open now↗ 02

Archery Akharaਤੀਰ ਅੰਦਾਜ਼ੀ

Learn the dhanush, the bow the Khalsa carried: stance, breath and the loose.Sundays at Guru Tegh Bahadur Gurdwara, East Park Road, for ages 14 and over. Open now↗ 03

Santhiya Vidyalaਸੰਥਿਆ ਵਿਦਿਆਲਾ

Learn to read Gurbani correctly: how each word is said, where to pause, and what it means.Every day at Gipsy Lane, and online. Open now↗ 04

Tabla & Joriਤਬਲਾ ਤੇ ਜੋੜੀ

Learn tabla and jori, and keep the taal that kirtan rests on.At all our gurdwaras. Open now↗ 05

Katha Vidyalaਕਥਾ ਵਿਦਿਆਲਾ

Learn to explain Gurbani in katha.What a shabad says, and how to say it to a sangat. Open now↗ 06

Itihaas Vidyalaਇਤਿਹਾਸ ਵਿਦਿਆਲਾ

Learn the history of the Panth.The sakhian, the shaheeds, and the bungas these classes are named after · Wednesday evenings Open now↗

How to start

Your first week.

There is no form to fill in and no waiting list.

੧

Send us a message.

Tell us which class you'd like, or that you're not sure yet. One of the teachers will reply with this week's times.

੨

Come along.

Arrive a few minutes early and cover your head. If you don't have an instrument, we'll lend you one, and granths are shared.

੩

Keep coming.

You learn at your own pace, from people who learnt the same way.

Ask us

Not sure where to start?

Santhiya is where every other class begins, because they all rest on reading Gurbani well. Send us a message and we'll help you choose.
