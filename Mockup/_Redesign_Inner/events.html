Through the yearThe year at Shaheedi Bunga

Most of what is in the archive below comes round every year. This is roughly when.

February to May. Gurbani Santhiya Camps at Gurudwara Shri Guru Dashmesh Sahib in the half-term and Easter holidays: four or five days of santhiya, morning or afternoon. There was a camp in December 2025 too.

March. The Patshahi Dasvi National Hola Mohalla UK: gatka and tag archery tournaments, war games, and the bhog of Sri Dasam Guru Granth Sahib Ji. It was at Gurdwara Gur Panth Parkash in 2025 and at Gipsy Lane on 7 March 2026.

The school holidays. Sikhi Camp for children aged 5 to 18, at whichever gurdwara is hosting. Contact us to find out more.

August. The Gurbani Vidhya Camp: two weeks at Guru Tegh Bahadur Gurdwara reading one granth through, every August since 2022.

Late November or early December. The 25 Singh Lareevaar Sri Akhand Paath Samagam, over three days. In 2021 it commemorated the Shaheedi Purab of Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji and the Gurgaddi Purab of Sri Guru Gobind Singh Ji. It has been held every winter since then, most recently with the bhog on 30 November 2025.

Once, or as a seriesTournaments and talks

Some events in the archive happened once, or ran as a single series.

The first UK inter-akhara Gatka Fight Night was held at Guru Tegh Bahadur Gurdwara on 4 May 2024, run with the Shaheed Bhai Mani Singh Ji Gatka Akhara. It included an exhibition match between two ustads and three fights between students of the akhare.

The National Sikh Archery Tournament was held at Guru Tegh Bahadur Gurdwara on 15 February 2025, in two classes: ages 10 to 15 shooting at 10 metres, and 16 and over at 15 metres.

Monday Night Talks ran through the spring of 2025, with pizza langar afterwards. Our own teachers gave some of the talks and speakers from other Sikh organisations gave the rest. Contact us to find out more.

On YouTubeWatch the samagams live

If you can't be in Leicester, the larger samagams are streamed on our YouTube channel from the first day to the bhog: the 25 Singh Lareevaar Sri Akhand Paath Samagam each winter, and Hola Mohalla UK in 2024 and 2025. Recorded talks and the katha of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji are there too.
