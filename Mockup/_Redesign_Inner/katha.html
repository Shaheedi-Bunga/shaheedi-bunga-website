Katha Vidyala

Learn to give Katha, and to explain Gurbani clearly to a Sangat.

WhereLeicester, worldwide & onlineFeeNone

What is taught

Guru Ram Das Ji says that as the earth is adorned when rain falls on it, so a Sikh comes into bloom on meeting Guru Sahib Ji. Katha asks for that kind of listening. A Shabad is opened one word at a time and explained plainly to the Sangat, until the meaning reaches each person the way rain reaches a field.

You learn to read a Shabad closely, work out what it is saying, and explain it to a Sangat plainly and correctly.

In 2019 students were learning Katha of Sri Gur Partap Suraj Granth with us, and students from the class gave Katha on stage.

Listen anywhereKatha of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji, Ang by Ang.

Larivaar Katha of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji goes out on our YouTube channel almost every day. Each episode carries on from the Ang where the last one stopped. Episode 284, on 27 October 2026, reached Ang 571.

The channel also has Katha of other Granths, including Adhyatam Parkash, added in September 2026.

If you live too far away to come to a class, start with these recordings.
