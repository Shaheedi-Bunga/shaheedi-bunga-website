Katha Vidyala

Expounding Gurbani: what the shabad says, and how to say it to a sangat.

When
Contact us to inquire
Where
At all our gurdwaras
Fee
None

What is taught

How to read a shabad closely, find what it is saying, and give that to a sangat plainly and correctly. Contact us to inquire more about who the class is for and who teaches it.

From the record

  • Gurbani Katha Vichaar (Larivaar Gurbani and Itihaas Katha) by Giani Baljinder Singh Ji (Shaheedi Bunga UK): Guru Arjan Dev Ji Gurdwara, Derby, 17 to 23 June 2019, and Sri Guru Har Rai Sahib Gurdwara Ji, West Bromwich, 24 to 30 June 2019.
  • Katha workshops by Gursikh vidhvaans at the Gurbani Vidhya Camp, 7 to 20 August 2023, Gurdwara Guru Tegh Bahadur, 106 East Park Road.
  • Katha Hazari: Katha Vichar by Sant Giani Inderjeet Singh Ji Raqbe Wale, 28 to 30 June 2023, 7pm, Gurdwara Guru Tegh Bahadur Sahib Ji, 106 East Park Road.
