Katha Vidyala

Learn to give katha: to understand what a shabad says and explain it clearly to a sangat.

WhenAsk usWhereAll our gurdwarasFeeNone

What is taught

You learn to read a shabad closely, work out what it is saying, and explain it to a sangat plainly and correctly.

Sri Gur Partap Suraj Granth is Kavi Santokh Singh Ji's granth of the lives and teachings of the Guru Sahiban. In 2019 students were learning katha of this granth with us, and students from the class gave katha on stage.

Contact us to ask who the class is for and who teaches it.

Listen anywhereKatha of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji, ang by ang.

Larivaar katha of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji goes out on our YouTube channel almost every day. Each episode carries on from the ang where the last one stopped. Episode 284, on 27 October 2026, reached Ang 571.

The channel also has katha of other granths, including Adhyatam Parkash, added in September 2026.

If you live too far away to come to a class, this is the place to start.
