Raag Explorer · ਰਾਗ ਖੋਜ

Privacy
policy.

The Raag Explorer app does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Last updated 12 November 2026

What stays on your phone

The raags you save as favourites are kept only on your phone. If you uninstall the app, they are removed with it. If your phone’s own backup (iCloud or Google) is switched on, it may include them; that backup is between you and Apple or Google, and the charity never sees it.

Sharing

When you share a raag, the picture and text go only where you send them, through your phone’s own share menu. The app does not see or keep a copy.

Internet

The app works entirely without an internet connection and sends nothing anywhere. The Android version does not even ask for internet access.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
