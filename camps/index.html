Camps

A fortnight, not an hour.

The weekly classes keep going all year. The camps are the other thing the vidyala does: whole days and whole weeks given to one subject, with teachers who come in for it. They are free, like everything else, and langar runs throughout.

01

ਗੁਰਬਾਣੀ ਵਿੱਦਿਆ ਕੈਂਪGurbani Vidhya Camp

A two-week intensive in the summer, and the most serious teaching the charity does. One granth is taken and worked through properly over the fortnight.

Where
Guru Tegh Bahadur Gurdwara
When
Two weeks each August
For
Older students and adults

02

ਗੁਰਬਾਣੀ ਸੰਥਿਆ ਕੈਂਪGurbani Santhiya Camp

Several days of santhiya in a row, morning or afternoon, with nothing else in the way. It is the quickest a reader moves: a week of this is worth a term of Tuesdays.

Where
Gurudwara Shri Guru Dashmesh Sahib
When
Several times a year, in the holidays
For
Any age, any level

03

ਸਿੱਖੀ ਕੈਂਪSikhi Camp

The children's camp, and the biggest thing the vidyala runs by numbers. Whole days of itihaas, kirtan, games and langar, pitched at children from about five upwards.

Where
Moves between the gurdware
When
School holidays
For
Children from 5

04

ਹੋਲਾ ਮਹੱਲਾHola Mohalla UK

The national gathering the charity hosts: gatka, archery, horse riding, axe and chakar throwing, rifle shooting. A day of shastar vidya rather than a class.

Where
Leicester: the venue changes
When
Around Hola Mohalla, in March
For
Everyone
