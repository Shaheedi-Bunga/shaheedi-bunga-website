Scroll

Guru Amar Das Ji calls the Shabad a lamp that lights all three worlds. Santhiya is learning to carry that lamp without letting it flicker. Every lagga matra is part of the flame, and a pause in the wrong place can dim a whole line. You sit with an ustad. They read a line, you read it back, and you move on only when it burns clear.

What Santhiya is

ਸੰਥਿਆ

Santhiya is learning to read Gurbani the way it was given. You sit with an ustad. They read a line, you read it back, and you move on only when it is right. Every other class at the Vidyala begins here.

Before you come

Anyone can start.

੨

Is there an audition?

No. Nobody tests you before you start.

੩

Is there an age limit?

No. Children of six and grandparents of seventy read from the same Pothi in the same room.

੪

Do I need to read Gurmukhi?

No. You don't need to know a single letter before you start.
