What santhiya is

ਸੰਥਿਆ

Santhiya is Gurbani read the way it was given. You sit with an ustad, they read a line, you read it back, and nothing moves on until it is right. It is where everything else at the vidyala starts.

Before you come

Nothing stands in the way.

Four things people ask before their first morning. The answer to all of them is the same.

੧

No fee.

Not for the class, not for the gutka, not later. The sangat keeps it running.

੨

No audition.

Nobody tests you before you start. You come, you sit, you begin where you are.

੩

No age.

Children of six and grandparents of seventy read from the same pothi in the same room.

੪

No Gurmukhi needed.

You do not need to read a single letter before you start. Sit at the back if you want to. Nobody will ask you to read.

The room

Fifteen years of mornings.

Camps, weekly classes and saptah paath. The same room, filling and emptying, for as long as anyone can remember.
