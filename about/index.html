Shaheedi Bunga

How it beganRegistered in 2019.

From 12 to 14 July 2019, students of the Shaheedi Bunga Gurmat Vidyala took part in a Sri Akhand Paath Sahib at Gipsy Lane carried out by 25 Singhs. A few weeks later, on 30 August 2019, the charity was registered.

What it doesWhat the charity is for.

Shaheedi Bunga is registered with the Charity Commission for four purposes: education and training, the prevention or relief of poverty, religious activities, and amateur sport.

In practice, that means classes, camps and samagams in gurdware around Leicester. The classes run every week at two gurdware. The camps run in the school holidays, and the Sikhi Camp for children is the largest of them. The samagams include Hola Mohalla UK each March and the 25 Singh Lareevaar Sri Akhand Paath Samagam each winter.

Who it is forEveryone is welcome.

The charity works for children and young people, elderly people, people with disabilities, other charities and voluntary bodies, and the general public. Nobody is turned away, and we charge nothing.

How it is runRun by volunteers, in the gurdware.

Shaheedi Bunga is a Charitable Incorporated Organisation (CIO), registered on 30 August 2019 and up to date with its reporting. It owns no buildings and leases none. The gurdware give us the space.

WhereTwo gurdware in Leicester, and online.

Guru Tegh Bahadur Gurdwara

106 East Park Road. Santhiya, tabla, and the Archery Akhara on Sundays.

Gurudwara Shri Guru Dashmesh Sahib

49 Gipsy Lane. The Kirtan Vidyala with nine classes a week, tabla and jori, and santhiya every day.

Online

Santhiya classes also run online. On YouTube, the katha of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji goes up almost every day, and the larger samagams are streamed live. Camps and samagams are held at different venues.

OnlineBeyond Leicester

Most people who follow our work never come to Leicester. The YouTube channel has more than 1,000 public videos, and of the people who watch them, 64% are in the UK and 32% in India. Live katha and samagams make up most of what they watch.
